About

Focused on leveraging agentic AI and emerging technologies to design, build, and deploy scalable intelligent systems.

I'm a forever learner and a builder at heart. My first interaction with programming was in the seventh grade, with an Arduino, and it changed the way I viewed the world and the scale of the problems I could solve. I had that experience again when I learned Python a few years later, and once more with the arrival of LLMs and agents.

The habit has never changed: I learn by building, then make it better. Automated gardens, a rover, a room full of homemade automation, an AI star classifier, and now agentic AI systems and cloud tooling.

My mission is to leverage the technology at hand to do something good, to make the world a better place, as cliché as it sounds, one step at a time.
